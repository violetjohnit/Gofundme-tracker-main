SMALL TRADER

WHAT IS IT?

SMALL TRADER is a crowdfunding platform that helps local small traders:
- to raise money to start,
- to restart their business
- to grow their businesses.

WHY?

Many small traders struggle to access traditional loans or enough capital to keep their businesses running. Small Trader gives them another way to get support directly from their community.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Small traders & micro-entrepreneurs who need funding.
Individuals who want to support local businesses.

HOW DOES IT WORK?

1. A trader creates a campaign
2. Explains their business and funding need
3. Sets a target
4. Receives contributions from supporters

Small Trader connects 2 PERSONALITY 
1. People who need business funding
2. People willing to help
