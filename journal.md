Small Trader - Development Journal | Version 1

Date: 30 August 2026
Version: V1 - Project Idea

Today, I defined the idea for Small Trader, a crowdfunding web application for local small traders and micro-entrepreneurs.

The idea is to create a platform where traders can create fundraising campaigns and receive financial support from individuals who want to help them start, restart, or grow their businesses.

The main problem I want to address is access to small amounts of business capital. Many local traders may have viable businesses but struggle to access traditional funding.

The initial focus is therefore on building a simple platform that connects traders who need funding with people willing to support them.

Initial target users:

Local small traders
Micro-entrepreneurs
Individuals who want to support small businesses
